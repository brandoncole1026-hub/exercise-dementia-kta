THE BIG PICTURE

Exercise has a role.
The goal matters.

Physical performance showed the clearest benefits in the project review. Improvements in thinking and memory were less predictable, so we should make careful, person-centered claims.

MOST CONSISTENT

Physical function

Strength, balance, aerobic capacity, and gait are important targets for exercise-based care.
