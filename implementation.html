03 / FROM KNOWLEDGE TO ACTION

Making evidence part of care

A practical translation pathway based on your project proposal—built for staff learning, shared decision-making, and measurable adoption.

IMPLEMENTATION PATHWAY

Four steps from
evidence to behavior.

This is a proposed rollout and evaluation plan from the submitted Knowledge-to-Action document. It is not a statement that facility approvals or clinical practice changes have already occurred.

01

PREPARE

Build understanding & support

Share an accessible summary of the review with departmental leadership, supervisors, and physical therapists. Invite discussion about relevance to existing practice.

Leadership review & clinician education
02

TAILOR

Identify barriers and adapt

Use clinician feedback to understand time, staffing, safety, setting, and patient-specific barriers. Tailor aerobic, strength, balance, and gait activities to the person's needs.

Clinical fit & safety adaptation
03

IMPLEMENT

Use evidence in everyday treatment

Integrate appropriate exercise categories into plans of care, use consistent cueing, and document interventions through existing facility processes.

Treatment planning & documentation
04

EVALUATE & SUSTAIN

Measure adoption over time

Compare baseline and follow-up clinician surveys and aggregate EMR documentation rates; discuss barriers and refine education if uptake decreases.

Quality improvement & follow-up

EVALUATION PLAN

What will change—and
how will we know?

BEFORE ROLLOUT

Baseline

Collect self-reported clinician practice patterns and the percentage of eligible EMR records documenting selected exercise interventions.

AT 12 WEEKS

Initial adoption

Repeat clinician surveys and audit the same selected documentation measures for eligible patients treated by participating clinicians.

12 WEEKS LATER

Sustainability

Repeat the EMR audit to see whether documented adoption has increased, stayed stable, or decreased; address emerging barriers.

Privacy by design: This public website does not collect patient information, store clinician survey responses, or connect to an EMR. Any actual clinical audit should follow institutional approval, privacy policy, and appropriate aggregate reporting.

SUPPORTING LONG-TERM CHANGE

Make it easier to sustain.

The project proposes education, recognition, and collaboration to help support practice change over time.

Explore teaching resources

Professional development

Explore education sessions and potential CEU options if approved and eligible.

Recognize participation

Acknowledge clinicians who consistently implement appropriate interventions.

Clinician ownership

Invite feedback and shared solutions to barriers within the care setting.
